🏆 MyDailyWin

📱 Add to Your Home Screen

Follow these steps to put MyDailyWin on your phone — just like a regular app!

⚠️ Important: Use Safari!

On iPhones, you must use the Safari browser (the blue compass icon). This won't work from Chrome or other browsers on iPhone.

1 Open Safari
Find the Safari app on your phone. It's the blue compass icon 🧭 that came with your phone.
2 Go to the website
In the address bar at the top, type:
my-daily-win.web.app
Then tap Go on the keyboard.
3 Tap the Share button
Look at the bottom of the screen. Find the Share button — it looks like a square with an arrow pointing up:
⬆️📤
Tap it.
4 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
A menu will pop up. Scroll down in that menu until you see:
➕ Add to Home Screen
Tap it.
5 Tap "Add"
A screen will appear showing the app name. Just tap Add in the top right corner. That's it!

🎉 You're done!

MyDailyWin is now on your home screen. Tap the icon anytime to open it — just like any other app!

💡 Tip: Use Chrome

On Android phones, use Chrome (the round colorful icon). It will show you an easy install button.

1 Open Chrome
Find the Chrome app on your phone. It's the round icon with red, yellow, green, and blue colors.
2 Go to the website
In the address bar at the top, type:
my-daily-win.web.app
Then tap Go or the arrow.
3 Look for the install banner
You might see a banner at the bottom that says "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App". If you see it, tap it and skip to Step 5!

If you don't see the banner, go to Step 4.
4 Tap the three dots menu
Look in the top right corner of Chrome. Tap the three dots:
⋮
Then look for "Add to Home screen" or "Install app" and tap it.
5 Tap "Install" or "Add"
A popup will appear. Tap Install or Add. That's it!

🎉 You're done!

MyDailyWin is now on your home screen. Tap the icon anytime to open it — just like any other app!

❓ Need help?

If you're having trouble, ask a family member to help you follow these steps. It only takes a minute!
