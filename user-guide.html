🏆 MyDailyWin

👋 Welcome!

This app gives you a short to-do list each day. Check things off, earn points, get paid real money. That's it!

📱 How it works

  1. Open the app — it's the green trophy 🏆 on your phone.
  2. See your tasks — a few simple things like "go for a walk" or "do a crossword."
  3. Tap the checkmark ✓ when you've done one.
  4. Earn points — 100 points = $1.00. Cash out anytime.

🔥 Streaks = bonus money

Do at least one task a day to keep your streak going.

  • 7 days in a row: earn 1.5x points on everything 🔥
  • 14 days in a row: earn 2x points on everything 🔥🔥

Missed a day? No worries — tap the streak badge, call a family member, and your streak comes right back.

💵 Getting paid

When you're ready to cash out:

  1. Tap the ⚙️ gear icon
  2. Tap Redeem Points
  3. Money comes to you via Zelle!

No deadline — cash out whenever you want.

💡 Good to know

  • Everything is optional. Skip whatever you want. No pressure.
  • The money comes from your kids. They set this up for you!
  • Nobody's watching you. Only you see the app on your phone.
  • You can't mess anything up. Tap checkmarks — that's all there is to it.
