🏆 MyDailyWin

👋 Welcome!

Your family set up a fun little app for you. It takes 2 minutes a day and you get paid for doing things you already do.

🤔 What is this?

MyDailyWin is a simple app on your phone. Every day, it shows you a short list of easy things to do — like going for a walk, doing a crossword, or playing Wordle.

When you do them, you tap a checkmark. You earn points. Points turn into real money.

That's it. No catch. Your family set this up because they want to keep you active, sharp, and having fun.

📱 Your daily routine (2 minutes)

  1. Open the app on your phone. (It's the green trophy icon 🏆 on your home screen.)
  2. Look at your tasks. There are usually 3–4 simple things to do today.
  3. Do the tasks whenever you feel like it during the day. No rush.
  4. Tap the checkmark ✓ next to each one when you've done it.
  5. Spin the wheel 🎰 after finishing all your daily tasks — it's a free bonus!
💡

You don't have to do everything. Even checking off one task counts. But the more you do, the more you earn — and the longer your streak gets!

🔥 What's a streak?

A streak means you've done at least one task every day in a row.

Why it matters:

  • Days 1–6: Normal points
  • Days 7–13: Everything earns 1.5x more! 🔥
  • Day 14+: Everything earns 2x more! 🔥🔥

So a 25-point walk becomes 37 points after a week, and 50 points after two weeks. It adds up!

😅 Missed a day? Don't worry — tap the streak badge and call a family member. Log the call, and your streak comes right back. We all have off days!

🏆 Weekly challenges

Each week, you'll see a couple of bigger challenges worth more points. Things like:

  • 🎾 Play tennis or pickleball (3 times = 90 pts)
  • 💑 Take your wife to lunch (500 pts!)
  • 📞 Call a friend or family member (200 pts)
  • 🍽️ Try a new restaurant (300 pts)

These are optional — but they're fun and worth a lot of points.

💵 Getting paid

Your points add up over time. When you're ready to cash out:

  1. Go to Settings (the ⚙️ gear icon)
  2. Tap Redeem Points
  3. Your family will send the money via Zelle

There's no deadline. Cash out whenever you want.

❓ You might be thinking...

"I don't need an app to tell me to take a walk."
You're right — you don't. But this isn't about being told what to do. It's about having a fun little thing to check in with each day. Think of it like a crossword — you do it because it's a nice part of your routine, not because someone's making you. Plus, you get paid. 😄
"Where does the money come from?"
From your kids! They set up a fund specifically for this. They want to reward you for staying active and having fun. It's their way of saying "we're proud of you, keep it up." It doesn't cost you anything.
"I'm not good with technology."
That's totally fine. All you do is open the app and tap checkmarks. There's nothing to type, no passwords to remember, no accounts to create. If you can tap a button on your phone, you can use this. And your family can help anytime.
"I'll forget to use it."
Most people just open it with their morning coffee. It takes 30 seconds to look at the list. Then check things off throughout the day. If you forget a day, no big deal — your streak can be recovered with a phone call to family. We built it to be forgiving.
"This seems too complicated."
It's simpler than it looks. Seriously — open the app, see a list, tap the green checkmarks. That's the whole thing. Ignore everything else if you want. The streaks, levels, and bonuses are just extra fun — you don't need to understand them to earn points.
"I don't want to be tracked or monitored."
Nobody's watching you. The app runs on your phone and only you see it. Your family can see your points total if you want (so they know when to pay you!), but they can't see what you're doing minute by minute. It's like a scoreboard, not a camera.
"What if I don't want to do a task?"
Skip it! Everything is optional. The tasks change every day, so tomorrow might have something better. Do what you enjoy, skip what you don't. There's no punishment for skipping — just fewer points that day.
