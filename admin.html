❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💰 How does the point system work?
100 points = $1.00

User earns points from:
  • Daily Tasks – 3 core tasks every day (Walk, Crossword, Wordle)
  • Daily Bonus – 1 rotating bonus task each day
  • Weekly Challenges – Bigger goals for extra points
  • Spin Wheel – Bonus spin after completing daily tasks
🔥 How do streaks work?
Complete at least one task per day to build a streak:
Days 1-6:Normal points (1x)
Days 7-13:🔥 1.5x multiplier on ALL tasks
Days 14+:🔥🔥 2x multiplier on ALL tasks

⚠️ Missing a day resets the streak to 0!

🎰 What is the spin wheel?
When User completes all 3 daily tasks, a bonus spin wheel unlocks!
  • One free spin per day
  • Prizes range from 10 to 150 points
  • No approval needed – it's automatic
  • Jackpot (150 pts) is rare but exciting! 🎉
🍀 What is a "Lucky Day"?
Each day has a 10% random chance of being a Lucky Day!
  • All tasks worth 1.5x points
  • Stacks with streak bonuses
  • Green "Lucky Day" banner appears at top of app
😴 What happens if User misses a day?
  • Streak resets to 0 (loses multiplier)
  • Daily tasks reset (can't complete yesterday's)
  • No penalty – just missed opportunity for points
  • Weekly challenges still available until week ends

💡 The app sends a login bonus (+25 pts) each day to encourage opening it!

✏️ How do I add, edit, or remove tasks?
Go to the Tasks tab:
  • Add: Click the "+ Add" button under any section
  • Edit: Click any task row → change name/points
  • Delete: Click task → "Delete Task" button
  • Move: Click task → change Category dropdown

Changes take effect immediately!

💵 How do payouts work?
  • Points accumulate as tasks are completed (100 pts = $1.00)
  • User requests a payout via the Cash Out button
  • Request appears in your Payments tab with a notification
  • Transfer funds to MyDailyWin → Payment sent via Zelle
  • Click "Mark as Sent" when complete
  • User gets a celebration notification in their app!

💡 Tip: No monthly deadline — they cash out when they're ready! Payments show as "from MyDailyWin" via Zelle.

🚩 What if User reports a task?
User can tap ••• on any bonus task to report it if they don't like it.
  • Reports appear in the Reports tab
  • Review the reason they gave
  • Edit or remove the task from Tasks tab
🔄 Can User recover a lost streak?
Yes! If User misses a day and their streak is at risk:
  • They can tap the streak badge on the main screen
  • A "Streak Recovery" option will appear
  • To recover, they must call a family member
  • They enter who they called, and the streak is restored!

💡 This encourages family connection while being forgiving about missed days.

🏆 How do levels work?
Levels are based on total lifetime points earned (not current balance).
  • 10 levels from Beginner to Hall of Fame
  • User can tap their level badge to see all levels
  • Levels never go down, even after payouts

See Levels tab for full breakdown!
