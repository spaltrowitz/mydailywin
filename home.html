📊 Quick Reference

💰 How does the point system work?
100 points = $1.00

Earn points from daily tasks, bonus challenges, weekly goals, and the spin wheel after completing your daily habits.
🔥 How do streaks work?
Complete at least one task per day to build a streak:

• Days 1-6: Normal points (1x)
• Days 7-13: 🔥 1.5x multiplier
• Days 14+: 🔥🔥 2x multiplier

⚠️ Missing a day resets the streak to 0!
🎰 What is the spin wheel?
Complete all your daily tasks to unlock a bonus spin! Prizes range from 10 to 150 points. One free spin per day, no approval needed.
💵 How do payouts work?
Points accumulate as tasks are completed. Cash out anytime via Settings → Redeem Points. No monthly deadline — redeem when you're ready!
🔄 Can I recover a lost streak?
Yes! If you miss a day, tap the streak badge. To recover, call a family member and log the call. This keeps you connected while being forgiving about missed days.
🏆 How do levels work?
Levels are based on total lifetime points earned (not current balance). There are 10 levels from Beginner to Hall of Fame. Levels never go down, even after payouts!
